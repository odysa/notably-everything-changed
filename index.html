Method

How this was measured

Corpus. OSDI, NSDI, MLSys, VLDB, NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR and ACL, each with a 2019–20 baseline and a 2024–26 sample (full proceedings for USENIX venues and MLSys; random samples of 150–200 otherwise). Six venues also have a 2016 control. ACL and ICML have 80 random papers for every year from 2021 to 2024. COLM (founded 2024) and EuroSys (ACM DL blocks scripts) use arXiv versions and are reported separately. NeurIPS uses 2024 because the 2025 main track was not yet posted.

Text. Body prose only: references, captions, headers, appendices and paragraphs under 40 words are dropped. Comparisons stay within a venue, so template differences cancel.

Attribution. A change counts as AI-era only when it did not happen between 2016 and 2020, breaks after 2022 in the yearly series, and holds in papers that are not about LLMs. Structure changes such as Limitations sections, longer PDFs and more references trace to venue rules and older trends instead.

Caveats. This measures a shift in style, timed with LLMs. It cannot show that any individual paper was written with AI, and nothing here is a claim about a specific paper. Imitation, editing tools and changes in who writes CS papers could all contribute.
